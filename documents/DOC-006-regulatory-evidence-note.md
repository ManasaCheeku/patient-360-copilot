# Synthetic Regulatory Evidence Traceability Note

## Section 1 — Demonstration objective

This fictional regulatory-style document demonstrates evidence traceability for the Patient 360 prototype. It is not an actual regulation, policy, submission, or legal guidance.

## Section 2 — Citation expectation

An evidence-backed response should identify its source name and the relevant record or section, show an excerpt, and avoid asserting facts absent from the retrieved context.

## Section 3 — Human review

This prototype is designed for demonstration and requires appropriate human review before any use beyond synthetic data.
