# Synthetic Medication Safety Bulletin: Monitoring Prompts

## Section 1 — Scope

This fictional educational bulletin illustrates how a copilot can connect a medication list to a safety-review prompt. It is not medical advice, prescribing information, or a substitute for a clinician or pharmacist.

## Section 2 — Anticoagulant review prompt

For a synthetic record containing apixaban, surface a medication-reconciliation prompt and review the latest documented renal function and bleeding-related symptoms with the responsible clinician. Do not infer that an adverse event occurred from medication presence alone.

## Section 3 — Insulin review prompt

For a synthetic record containing insulin, surface a prompt to verify that the person has received appropriate individualized education about administration and low-glucose symptoms. The presence of insulin does not prove hypoglycemia.

## Section 4 — Methotrexate review prompt

For a synthetic record containing methotrexate, surface a prompt to verify routine treatment monitoring is documented. This prototype does not determine whether treatment is appropriate.

## Section 5 — NSAID review prompt

For a synthetic record containing regular or as-needed naproxen, surface a prompt to review use, gastrointestinal history, and renal monitoring in the clinical workflow. This is a generic educational reminder, not a patient-specific conclusion.
